## 👋 Who are we?

Hej! 🇩🇰 We're **Psykoterapiforeningen**, a small team of Danish developers hanging out at **insanitysmp** 🎮.
Despite the name 😄🛋️, we don't do therapy here, we do **code**! 💻🔥

We love tinkering, breaking things, and shipping stuff that makes Minecraft a little more *insane* 🤪⛏️.
